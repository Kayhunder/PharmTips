About

Built by Clinicians.
For Clinicians.

Pharmmed is an independent clinical pharmacy and medical education blog dedicated to bridging the gap between pharmacological evidence and real-world patient care.

Mission

Evidence that works in the real world

Good pharmacotherapy information that's truly actionable is hard to find. Pharmmed exists to close that gap — delivering content that's both evidence-based and clinically practical.

The Author

Pharmmed Editorial

Pharmmed is written by a pharmacist trained at the School of Pharmacy, University of Nairobi, with a background in clinical pharmacy and health data analytics. Experience spans clinical practice, healthcare quality improvement, and health data work in resource-constrained settings across Kenya.

The focus is on content that reflects the realities of healthcare delivery in diverse clinical environments — not just high-resource Western hospital settings.

⚠️ Disclaimer — Educational Purposes Only

All content on Pharmmed is for educational and informational purposes only. Nothing constitutes medical advice or a substitute for professional judgment. Always verify with authoritative references and institutional protocols.
